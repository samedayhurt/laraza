# Data Broker Opt-Out Starter Kit

**The first 5 removals that matter most**

---

## Why This Matters

**750+ data brokers** sell your information. You can't opt out of all of them, but you can hit the ones that matter most.

These five sites are:
- Most commonly used for **stalking and doxing**
- Most likely to appear in **law enforcement searches**
- Easiest to opt out of (instant or 24-48 hours)

**Do these five. Repeat every 3 months.** (Your data will reappear.)

---

## Before You Start

### What You'll Need
- Your full name (and variations: maiden name, nicknames)
- Current and previous addresses
- Email address (for verification)
- Phone number (for some sites)
- 15-20 minutes

### What NOT to Provide
- **Social Security Number** — No legitimate opt-out requires this
- **Government ID photo** — Be suspicious if asked
- **Payment** — Opt-outs are always free

## Site 1: TruePeopleSearch

**Why it matters:** Extremely detailed, frequently used for doxing, instant removal

**Time to remove:** Instant

### Steps
1. Go to **truepeoplesearch.com**
2. Search your name + state
3. Click your listing
4. Scroll to bottom → **"Remove This Record"**
5. Confirm removal

**Note:** Data may reappear within weeks. Re-do this monthly.

---

## Site 2: FastPeopleSearch

**Why it matters:** Another major doxing source, instant removal

**Time to remove:** Instant

### Steps
1. Go to **fastpeoplesearch.com**
2. Search your name
3. Click your listing
4. Scroll down → **"Remove My Record"**
5. Complete CAPTCHA
6. Confirm

---

## Site 3: Spokeo

**Why it matters:** One of the oldest and most comprehensive people-search sites

**Time to remove:** 24-48 hours

### Steps
1. Go to **spokeo.com**
2. Search your name
3. Find your listing and **copy the full URL** (e.g., spokeo.com/John-Smith-123456789)
4. Go to **spokeo.com/optout**
5. Paste the URL
6. Enter your email
7. Click opt-out link in verification email

**Important:** You must paste the exact URL of your listing, not just search.

---

## Site 4: WhitePages

**Why it matters:** Legacy site with deep historical data, feeds other services

**Time to remove:** 24 hours

### Steps
1. Go to **whitepages.com**
2. Search your name
3. Find your listing → Click to open full profile
4. Copy the URL
5. Go to **whitepages.com/suppression-requests**
6. Paste URL, enter email, complete form
7. Verify via email
8. May require phone verification (automated call)

---

## Site 5: BeenVerified

**Why it matters:** Popular with employers, landlords, and investigators

**Time to remove:** 24 hours

### Steps
1. Go to **beenverified.com/f/optout/search**
2. Enter first name, last name, state
3. Find your listing → Click **"Proceed to opt out"**
4. Enter email
5. Click verification link
6. Complete removal

**Note:** This also covers PeopleLooker (same company).

## Protecting Your Location Data

These sites get data from public records. But **location brokers** get data from your phone. Different problem, different solution.

**Location data brokers you can't opt out of directly:**
- Venntel / Gravy Analytics (sells to ICE, CBP)
- Babel Street (Locate X)
- Fog Data Science (sells to 1,000+ local police)

**What you can do:**

1. **Audit app permissions**
   - iPhone: Settings → Privacy → Location Services
   - Android: Settings → Location → App permissions
   - Set everything to "Never" except maps

2. **Delete advertising ID**
   - iPhone: Settings → Privacy → Tracking → OFF
   - Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads → Delete advertising ID

3. **Delete apps you don't use**
   - Every app is a potential data leak

4. **Turn off WiFi/Bluetooth when not using**
   - Passive scanning reveals location even without GPS

## The Reality

**You cannot fully disappear.** Data brokers collect from public records, voter rolls, property records, court filings. Some of this is legally required to be public.

**What you can do:**
- Remove yourself from the most dangerous people-search sites
- Make it harder to compile a complete profile
- Cut off the location data pipeline from your phone
- Repeat regularly (this is maintenance, not a one-time fix)
