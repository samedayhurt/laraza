## Legal References

### 5th Amendment and Biometrics

| Case | Holding |
|------|---------|
| **State v. Diamond (MN 2017)** | Fingerprint can be compelled (not testimonial) |
| **Commonwealth v. Baust (VA 2014)** | Fingerprint compelled, passcode protected (testimonial) |
| **In re Search Warrant (N.D. Ill. 2017)** | Touch ID compulsion upheld |

**Takeaway:** Courts generally treat biometrics as physical keys (compellable), PINs as testimony (protected). Disable biometrics before sensitive situations.

### Recording Police

| Jurisdiction | Status |
|--------------|--------|
| **Colorado** | Legal to record police in public |
| **Federal (1st Circuit)** | Glik v. Cunniffe (2011) — Constitutional right to record |

**Practical:** You can record. Keep phone visible. Do not interfere. Back up footage immediately.

### ICE and Warrants

| Document | What It Means |
|----------|---------------|
| **Administrative warrant** | Signed by immigration officer — NOT sufficient to enter home |
| **Judicial warrant** | Signed by federal judge — Must comply |

**Practical:** Ask through closed door: "Do you have a warrant signed by a judge?" If no, you do not have to open.
