# Glossary of Key Terms

Quick reference for surveillance, privacy, and rights terminology used throughout this project.

---

## Surveillance Technology

| Term | Definition | Icon |
|------|------------|------|
| **ALPR** | Automatic License Plate Reader - cameras that capture and store license plates, often mounted on police cars or fixed poles | 🚗 |
| **RTCC** | Real-Time Crime Center - centralized hub where police monitor live camera feeds, ALPRs, and other surveillance data | 🖥️ |
| **ShotSpotter** | Acoustic gunshot detection system using microphones to triangulate gunfire; now called SoundThinking | 🔊 |
| **Community Connect** | Genetec program where businesses and residents voluntarily share private camera feeds with police | 📹 |
| **Flock Safety** | Private company providing networked ALPR cameras to police; shares data across 5,000+ agencies | 🦅 |
| **Stingray** | Cell-site simulator (CSS) that mimics cell towers to intercept phone data; also called IMSI catcher | 📡 |
| **IMSI** | International Mobile Subscriber Identity - unique number identifying your SIM card | 📱 |
| **IMEI** | International Mobile Equipment Identity - unique hardware ID for your phone | 📱 |
| **Remote ID** | FAA requirement (since March 2024) for drones to broadcast identification and location | ✈️ |
| **Geofence** | Virtual boundary used to track devices entering/leaving an area | 📍 |
| **Facial Recognition** | AI technology matching faces to identity databases; often biased against darker skin tones | 👤 |
| **Clearview AI** | Controversial company that scraped billions of social media photos for facial recognition database | 👁️ |

## Data & Privacy

| Term | Definition | Icon |
|------|------------|------|
| **Data Broker** | Company that collects and sells personal information (location, purchases, contacts) | 💰 |
| **Fog Data Science** | Data broker selling location data from apps to police; claims 250M+ devices tracked | 🌫️ |
| **Babel Street** | Company providing Locate X tool to track phones via commercial data; used by ICE/CBP | 🗼 |
| **Ad ID** | Advertising identifier on your phone that tracks you across apps; can be reset | 🎯 |
| **Telemetry** | Data automatically collected and transmitted by devices/apps about your behavior | 📊 |
| **Metadata** | Data about data - for photos: location, time, device; for calls: who, when, how long | 📋 |
| **EXIF** | Exchangeable Image File - hidden data in photos including GPS coordinates | 🖼️ |
| **E2EE** | End-to-End Encryption - only sender and recipient can read messages (Signal uses this) | 🔐 |
| **VPN** | Virtual Private Network - encrypts internet traffic and masks your IP address | 🛡️ |
| **Tor** | The Onion Router - anonymity network routing traffic through multiple relays | 🧅 |

---

## Immigration & Law Enforcement

| Term | Definition | Icon |
|------|------------|------|
| **ICE** | Immigration and Customs Enforcement - federal agency conducting immigration arrests | 🏛️ |
| **CBP** | Customs and Border Protection - operates at borders; 100-mile "border zone" powers | 🛂 |
| **287(g)** | Program where local police act as immigration agents (Pueblo County does NOT participate) | ⚠️ |
| **Detainer** | ICE request to hold someone after their release date; NOT mandatory in Colorado | 📋 |
| **A-Number** | Alien Registration Number - 8-9 digit ID for immigration cases | 🔢 |
| **CORRN** | Colorado Rapid Response Network - hotline for ICE emergencies: 844-864-8341 | ☎️ |
| **RMIAN** | Rocky Mountain Immigrant Advocacy Network - legal services for detained immigrants | ⚖️ |
| **Sanctuary** | Policy limiting local cooperation with federal immigration enforcement | 🏠 |
| **Warrant** | Court order signed by a judge; ICE administrative warrants are NOT the same | 📜 |

## Legal & Rights

| Term | Definition | Icon |
|------|------------|------|
| **FOIA** | Freedom of Information Act - federal law for requesting government records | 📄 |
| **CORA** | Colorado Open Records Act - state equivalent of FOIA | 📄 |
| **4th Amendment** | Constitutional protection against unreasonable searches and seizures | ⚖️ |
| **5th Amendment** | Right to remain silent and not incriminate yourself | 🤐 |
| **1st Amendment** | Rights to free speech, assembly, and press | 📢 |
| **One-Party Consent** | Colorado law: you can record conversations you're part of without telling others | 🎙️ |
| **Two-Party Consent** | Some states require ALL parties to consent to recording | ⚠️ |
| **Carpenter v. US** | 2018 Supreme Court ruling requiring warrants for historical cell location data | ⚖️ |

---

## Operational Security

| Term | Definition | Icon |
|------|------------|------|
| **OPSEC** | Operational Security - practices to protect sensitive information | 🔒 |
| **OSINT** | Open Source Intelligence - gathering info from public sources | 🔍 |
| **Threat Model** | Assessment of who might target you and what they could do | ⚠️ |
| **Compartmentalization** | Keeping different activities/identities separate | 📦 |
| **Faraday Bag** | Pouch that blocks all radio signals to/from devices inside | 📵 |
| **Burner Phone** | Disposable phone not linked to your identity | 📱 |
| **Air Gap** | Device never connected to internet; maximum security | 🔌 |
| **Dead Drop** | Location for leaving information without direct contact | 📬 |
| **Legal Observer** | Trained volunteer documenting police actions at protests | 👁️ |

---

## Technical Terms

| Term | Definition | Icon |
|------|------------|------|
| **VM** | Virtual Machine - computer running inside another computer for isolation | 💻 |
| **SDR** | Software Defined Radio - programmable radio receiver for monitoring signals | 📻 |
| **MAC Address** | Unique hardware identifier for network devices; can be randomized | 🔢 |
| **2FA/MFA** | Two-Factor/Multi-Factor Authentication - requiring multiple proofs of identity | 🔑 |
| **Biometric** | Authentication using body features (fingerprint, face) - can be compelled by police | 👆 |
| **PIN** | Personal Identification Number - cannot be compelled (5th Amendment protection) | 🔢 |
| **FERPA** | Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act - protects student education records | 🎓 |
| **COPPA** | Children's Online Privacy Protection Act - protects children under 13 online | 👶 |
