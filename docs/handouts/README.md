## Printing Instructions

### Wallet Cards (Emergency Contacts, Traffic Stop)
1. Print double-sided (flip on short edge)
2. Cut along marked lines
3. Fold to wallet size
4. Laminate if possible for durability

### Single-Page Documents (KYR-ICE)
1. Print single-sided or double-sided
2. Consider cardstock for durability
3. Distribute at community events, clinics, churches

### Multi-Page Guides (Post-Arrest Roadmap)
1. Print all pages
2. Staple or bind together
3. Keep copies at legal aid offices, bail funds, community centers

## Distribution Points

Good places to leave printed copies:
- Community health centers
- Legal aid offices
- Churches and faith organizations
- Libraries
- Community centers (El Centro del Quinto Sol)
- Laundromats (with permission)
- Immigration legal clinics

## Languages Available

- English
- Spanish

**Need translations to other languages?** Contact community organizations serving Vietnamese, Somali, or Burmese populations in Pueblo to coordinate translation efforts.

## Updates
