# KNOW YOUR RIGHTS: If ICE Comes to Your Door

**PUEBLO, CO - Updated November 2025**

---

## YOU HAVE THE RIGHT TO REMAIN SILENT

---

### IF SOMEONE KNOCKS ON YOUR DOOR

1. **DO NOT OPEN THE DOOR**
   - You have the right to keep your door closed
   - Speak through the door or window

2. **ASK: "Do you have a warrant signed by a judge?"**
   - If NO → Say: "I choose not to open the door"
   - If YES → Ask them to slide it under the door
   - A real warrant has a judge's signature and your correct address

3. **DO NOT SIGN ANYTHING**
   - Without talking to a lawyer first

4. **STAY CALM AND SILENT**
   - You have the right to remain silent
   - Say: "I am exercising my right to remain silent"

---

## IMPORTANT: NOVEMBER 2025 COURT RULING

**A federal judge ruled that ICE cannot make warrantless arrests in Colorado based solely on immigration status.**

If ICE tries to arrest you without a warrant, say:
> "A federal court has ruled ICE cannot make warrantless arrests in Colorado."
