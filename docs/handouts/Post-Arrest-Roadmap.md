# Post-Arrest Roadmap: What Happens After You're Arrested

A step-by-step guide for the first 72 hours after arrest in Pueblo, Colorado.

---

## Hour 0-2: Booking Process

### What Happens

```
BOOKING PROCESS
===============

1. TRANSPORT TO JAIL
   • Pueblo County Detention Center
   • 320 W. 10th St, Pueblo, CO 81003
   • You may be held in police vehicle first

2. INTAKE PROCESSING
   • Personal information taken
   • Photographed (mugshot)
   • Fingerprinted
   • Personal belongings inventoried and stored
   • Medical screening (basic health questions)
   • Classification assessment (housing assignment)

3. PHONE CALL
   • You have the right to a phone call
   • Know your emergency contact number BY HEART
   • Call a lawyer, family member, or bail fund
```

### Your Rights During Booking

| You CAN | You CANNOT |
|---------|------------|
| Make a phone call | Be denied access to phone indefinitely |
| Know your charges | Be questioned without Miranda rights |
| Request medical attention | Be strip-searched without cause |
| Stay silent beyond ID info | Be held without being informed of charges |

### What To Say / Not Say

- **DO give**: Name, date of birth, address
- **DON'T give**: Explanations, stories, "context" for what happened
- **SAY**: "I am exercising my right to remain silent. I want a lawyer."
- **REPEAT** if asked more questions

## Hours 2-24: Initial Custody

### Bond / Bail Information

**How Bail Works in Colorado:**

| Charge Type | Typical Bail | Notes |
|-------------|--------------|-------|
| Petty offense | Often PR bond (no cost) | Promise to appear |
| Misdemeanor | $100 - $2,000 | Cash or bond company |
| Low-level felony | $2,000 - $10,000 | May need bail bondsman |
| Serious felony | $10,000+ | May be denied bail |

**PR (Personal Recognizance) Bond**: Released on promise to appear. No money needed.

**Cash Bond**: Pay full amount; get it back when case concludes (minus fees).

**Bail Bondsman**: Pay 10-15% to bondsman (non-refundable); they post the rest.

### Getting Out

```
RELEASE OPTIONS
===============

1. PERSONAL RECOGNIZANCE (PR) BOND
   • No money required
   • Sign agreement to appear at court dates
   • Most common for minor charges / first offense

2. CASH BOND
   • Pay full bond amount to jail
   • Money returned after case concludes
   • Can take hours to process after payment

3. BAIL BONDSMAN
   • Pay 10-15% fee (non-refundable)
   • Bondsman posts rest
   • Available 24/7 in most areas

4. PROPERTY BOND
   • Use property as collateral
   • Less common; takes longer
```

### Conditions of Release

You may be required to:
- Check in with pretrial services
- Stay in Colorado / county
- Avoid certain people or places
- Submit to drug testing
- Wear GPS monitor (serious charges)

**CRITICAL**: Violating conditions = immediate re-arrest

## Hours 24-72: First Court Appearance

### Advisement Hearing (Arraignment)

This happens within **48 hours** of arrest (excluding weekends/holidays).

**At this hearing:**
1. Judge reads your charges
2. You enter a plea (say "Not Guilty")
3. Bond may be set or modified
4. Next court date scheduled
5. Public defender assigned if you can't afford attorney

### What To Do

```
AT ADVISEMENT HEARING
=====================

□ DRESS appropriately if possible (family can bring clothes)
□ STAND when judge addresses you
□ SAY "Not Guilty" when asked for plea
□ REQUEST public defender if needed
□ ASK for clarification on conditions of release
□ GET next court date in writing
□ DON'T discuss your case with anyone except your lawyer
```

### Public Defender

**If you can't afford an attorney:**

1. Request public defender at advisement
2. Fill out financial affidavit (income verification)
3. Office will contact you with attorney assignment
4. **Pueblo Public Defender Office**: 719-583-6900

**What public defenders do:**
- Review your case
- Advise you on options
- Represent you in court
- Negotiate with prosecutor
- File motions on your behalf

## After Release: Next Steps

### Immediate Priorities (First 24 hours out)

```
POST-RELEASE CHECKLIST
======================

□ DOCUMENT everything while fresh
   • Write down what happened leading to arrest
   • Names/badge numbers of officers involved
   • Time, location, witnesses
   • What you said (if anything)
   • How you were treated

□ PHOTOGRAPH any injuries
   • Bruises may take 24-48 hours to appear
   • Date-stamp photos
   • Store in multiple places

□ CONTACT your lawyer
   • If you have one already
   • Or wait for public defender assignment

□ GATHER witness information
   • Names and contact info
   • What they saw/heard
   • Their relationship to you (if any)

□ RETRIEVE your property
   • Get property receipt from jail
   • Pick up belongings during jail business hours
   • Note if anything is missing/damaged

□ TELL your employer (if needed)
   • Know your rights - some arrests are protected
   • Don't overshare details
```

### Court Dates - DO NOT MISS

**Failure to appear (FTA) = new warrant + additional charges**

| Action | When |
|--------|------|
| Mark ALL court dates on calendar | Immediately |
| Set multiple reminders | 1 week, 3 days, 1 day before |
| Arrange transportation | Day before |
| Plan for childcare if needed | In advance |
| Contact lawyer if conflict | Immediately |

## Filing Complaints

### If You Were Mistreated

**Step 1: Pueblo PD Internal Affairs**
- File within 60 days
- Online: pueblo.us/449/Internal-Affairs-Section
- Phone: 719-553-2412
- In person: 200 S. Main St

**Step 2: If IA Doesn't Act - Colorado POST**
- For officer decertification (serious misconduct)
- post.colorado.gov/certification-inquiries-officer-complaints

**Step 3: Colorado AG Pattern & Practice**
- For systemic civil rights violations
- coag.gov/office-sections/civil-rights/pattern-and-practice/
- Select "Governmental Authority"

**Step 4: Federal DOJ Civil Rights**
- For serious federal violations
- civilrights.justice.gov/report/

## Guía Rápida en Español / Spanish Quick Reference

### Si Es Arrestado:

1. **Diga**: "Estoy ejerciendo mi derecho a guardar silencio. Quiero un abogado."
2. **NO** responda preguntas sobre lo que pasó
3. **NO** firme nada sin abogado
4. **NO** resista físicamente

### Después de Ser Liberado:

1. **Escriba** todo lo que pasó mientras lo recuerda
2. **Fotografíe** cualquier lesión
3. **Contacte** un abogado o defensor público: 719-583-6900
4. **NO falte** a ninguna cita en la corte

### Contactos de Emergencia:

- **Colorado Legal Services**: 719-545-6708
- **ACLU Colorado**: 303-777-5482

## Know Your Timeline

```
CRITICAL DEADLINES
==================

ARREST
  │
  ├─ Within 48 hours ─────► Advisement hearing (first court appearance)
  │
  ├─ At advisement ────────► Enter "Not Guilty" plea
  │                         Request public defender if needed
  │
  ├─ 60 days from arrest ──► Deadline to file IA complaint
  │
  ├─ Ongoing ──────────────► ALL scheduled court dates (DO NOT MISS)
  │
  └─ Before trial ─────────► Pre-trial motions, discovery, negotiations
```

## What NOT To Do

```
COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID
========================

✗ DON'T talk to police without a lawyer
✗ DON'T discuss your case with other inmates
✗ DON'T post about your case on social media
✗ DON'T contact alleged victims or witnesses
✗ DON'T miss court dates (warrant issued immediately)
✗ DON'T violate conditions of release
✗ DON'T pay fines before consulting lawyer
✗ DON'T plead guilty at advisement - say "Not Guilty"
```
