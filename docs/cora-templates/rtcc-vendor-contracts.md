Under the Colorado Open Records Act, C.R.S. 24-72-200.1 et seq., I am
requesting copies of the following public records:

1. All current contracts, memoranda of understanding (MOUs), and service
   agreements between the City of Pueblo / Pueblo Police Department and
   vendors providing Real Time Crime Center (RTCC) services, including but
   not limited to:
   - SoundThinking (formerly ShotSpotter)
   - Genetec
   - High Point Networks
   - Daktronics
   - Any other surveillance technology or data analytics vendors

2. All invoices and payment records for RTCC-related services from
   January 1, 2020 to present.

3. Any data-sharing agreements or MOUs governing how RTCC data is shared
   with other law enforcement agencies (including federal agencies such as
   ICE, CBP, FBI, or DEA).

4. Training materials, policies, or standard operating procedures
   governing RTCC operations and data access.

5. Any audits, evaluations, or performance reports related to RTCC
   effectiveness or civil liberties compliance.

PREFERRED FORMAT: Electronic copies (PDF) sent via email are preferred.
If records exceed 50 pages, please provide an index/log first.

FEE WAIVER REQUEST: I request a waiver of fees pursuant to C.R.S.
24-72-205(6). Disclosure of these records is in the public interest
because [select one or both]:
  [ ] I am a journalist/researcher investigating public surveillance
      systems for publication
  [ ] The records concern government accountability and civil liberties
      protections that benefit the general public

If fees cannot be waived, please notify me if costs exceed $25 before
proceeding.

RESPONSE DEADLINE: Please respond within 3 working days as required by
C.R.S. 24-72-203(3)(b). If you need an extension, please provide written
notice within that period.

If any portion of this request is denied, please cite the specific
statutory exemption and provide a written explanation for each withheld
record.

## Follow-Up Actions

After submitting:

1. **Log the request** in `logs/search_log.md`:
   ```
   | 2025-XX-XX | CORA | RTCC contracts request to Pueblo PD | Submitted, awaiting response |
   ```

2. **Set calendar reminder** for Day 3 (initial response deadline)

3. **If no response by Day 3:**
   - Send follow-up email citing C.R.S. 24-72-203(3)(b)
   - Note: Agency may claim 7-day extension with written notice

4. **If denied:**
   - Request written explanation citing specific exemption
   - Consider appeal to district court within 14 days
   - Contact Colorado Freedom of Information Coalition for support

5. **When records received:**
   - Update `data/surveillance_policing_raw.md` with findings
   - Highlight contract terms, data retention, and sharing provisions
   - Flag any redactions for potential challenge

## What to Look For

- **Contract duration and renewal terms** - When do citizens get a say?
- **Cost per year** - Compare to community investment alternatives
- **Data retention periods** - How long is surveillance data kept?
- **Access controls** - Who can query the system? Any audit logs?
- **Federal sharing** - Is data shared with ICE, CBP, or fusion centers?
- **Performance metrics** - What outcomes justify the expense?
- **Privacy impact assessments** - Were any conducted before deployment?
