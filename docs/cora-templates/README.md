# CORA Request Templates

Colorado Open Records Act (C.R.S. 24-72-200.1 et seq.) templates for requesting surveillance and policing records from Pueblo agencies.

## Available Templates

| Template | Target Agency | Records Requested |
|----------|---------------|-------------------|
| [RTCC Vendor Contracts](rtcc-vendor-contracts.md) | Pueblo PD / City Purchasing | Real Time Crime Center contracts, invoices, data-sharing agreements |
| [ALPR Placement & Policies](alpr-placement-policies.md) | Pueblo PD / City | ALPR locations, retention policies, query logs (aggregate) |
| [Internal Affairs Statistics](internal-affairs-stats.md) | Pueblo PD | Complaint counts, dispositions, use-of-force incidents |
| [Community Connect Agreements](community-connect-agreements.md) | Pueblo PD / City IT | Private camera partnership MOUs, access policies, participating locations |

## How to Use

1. **Select template** matching the records you need
2. **Fill in** your name, contact info, and the date
3. **Send via email** to the designated records custodian (addresses in each template)
4. **Track response** - agencies have 3 working days to respond (extension up to 7 additional days permitted)
5. **Document** the request in `logs/search_log.md` with date, agency, and outcome

## CORA Basics

- **Who can request:** Any person (no residency requirement)
- **Response deadline:** 3 working days; agencies may extend 7 more with written notice
- **Fees:** Agencies may charge reasonable copying/research fees; request fee waiver if for public interest journalism/advocacy
- **Denials:** Must cite specific statutory exemption; you can appeal to district court within 14 days
- **Exemptions to watch:** Law enforcement investigation files (24-72-305), personnel files, trade secrets

## Fee Waiver Language

All templates include optional fee waiver language. Use if:
- You're a journalist or nonprofit researcher
- Records serve public interest (surveillance oversight, civil rights)
- You lack ability to pay
