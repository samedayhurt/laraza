## Background: What is Community Connect?

Community Connect is Pueblo PD's program enabling voluntary registration of private security cameras for police access during investigations. The program:

- Allows businesses and residents to register camera locations
- Creates a map of available footage sources for detectives
- May enable direct or expedited access to footage
- Integrates with Genetec VMS at the Real Time Crime Center

**Key concerns:**
- Scope of access (live feed vs. on-request?)
- Data retention (how long do recordings stay accessible?)
- Warrantless access provisions
- Expansion to residential neighborhoods

   b. Breakdown by participant type (business, residential, HOA,

3. ACCESS & USE POLICIES
   a. Policies governing when police may request footage from

   a. Any agreements allowing other agencies (federal, state, county)

## Advocacy Angles

**If warrant not required:**
- Advocate for council resolution requiring judicial authorization
- Cite 4th Amendment concerns and ACLU best practices

**If live feed access exists:**
- Demand transparency on which locations have live access
- Push for geographic limits (no residential live feeds)

**If federal sharing allowed:**
- Risk of ICE using camera network for immigration enforcement
- Cite Sheriff Lucero's cooperation limits as policy anchor

**If participation is expanding rapidly:**
- Request council oversight of geographic scope
- Propose notification requirements for affected neighborhoods
