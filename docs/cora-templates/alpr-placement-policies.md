```
RE: Colorado Open Records Act Request - Automated License Plate Readers

Dear Records Custodian:

Under the Colorado Open Records Act, C.R.S. 24-72-200.1 et seq., I am
requesting copies of the following public records related to Automated
License Plate Reader (ALPR) systems operated by or accessible to the
Pueblo Police Department:

1. ALPR DEPLOYMENT & LOCATIONS
   a. A map, list, or description of all fixed ALPR camera locations
      within Pueblo city limits and Pueblo County
   b. The number and deployment locations of mobile ALPRs (vehicle-mounted)
   c. Any planned expansion locations for ALPR deployment

2. VENDOR CONTRACTS & COSTS
   a. Current contracts with Flock Safety, Genetec, Vigilant Solutions,
      or other ALPR vendors
   b. Total annual costs for ALPR systems (hardware, software, maintenance)
   c. Any HOA, business, or private entity agreements that feed data into
      police-accessible ALPR networks

3. POLICIES & PROCEDURES
   a. Standard operating procedures governing ALPR data collection,
      access, and use
   b. Data retention policies (how long plate reads are stored)
   c. Policies governing "hot list" alerts and officer response protocols
   d. Any policies restricting ALPR use for immigration enforcement

4. DATA ACCESS & SHARING
   a. List of agencies with access to Pueblo ALPR data (including federal
      agencies, fusion centers, and other law enforcement)
   b. Any data-sharing agreements or MOUs governing ALPR data exchange
   c. Aggregate statistics on ALPR queries by agency (monthly/annual)

5. AGGREGATE USE STATISTICS (not individual records)
   a. Total plate reads per month for the past 12 months
   b. Total "hot list" alerts generated per month
   c. Number of arrests or citations directly resulting from ALPR alerts

I am NOT requesting individual plate reads, specific investigative
records, or information that would identify individual vehicles or
persons.

PREFERRED FORMAT: Electronic copies (PDF) via email.

FEE WAIVER REQUEST: I request a waiver of fees pursuant to C.R.S.
24-72-205(6). Disclosure serves the public interest by enabling citizen
oversight of surveillance technology that affects all drivers in Pueblo.

If fees cannot be waived, please notify me if costs exceed $25.

RESPONSE DEADLINE: Please respond within 3 working days per C.R.S.
24-72-203(3)(b).

If any portion is denied, please cite the specific statutory exemption
and provide written explanation.

Thank you for your assistance.

Sincerely,

[YOUR NAME]
[YOUR SIGNATURE]
```

## Red Flags to Document

- **No written policy** - Violation of best practices; cite EFF/ACLU guidelines in advocacy
- **Indefinite retention** - Disproportionate to law enforcement need
- **Federal sharing without limits** - Risk of immigration enforcement misuse
- **No audit logs** - Impossible to verify appropriate use
- **HOA/private feeds** - Expanding surveillance without council approval
