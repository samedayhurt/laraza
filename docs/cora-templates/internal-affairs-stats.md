## Context: Colorado POST Requirements

Since SB20-217 (Enhance Law Enforcement Integrity), Colorado requires:
- **Body camera footage** retention and disclosure rules
- **POST decertification** database for officers terminated for cause
- **Duty to intervene** when witnessing excessive force
- **Ban on chokeholds** except when deadly force is justified

Use CORA responses to verify Pueblo PD compliance with these mandates.

## Analyzing the Response

### Metrics to Calculate

| Metric | Formula | Benchmark |
|--------|---------|-----------|
| Sustained rate | Sustained / Total complaints | National avg: 8-12% |
| Discipline rate | Officers disciplined / Sustained complaints | Should be ~100% |
| Force rate | UOF incidents / Total arrests | Compare to peer cities |
| Complaint per officer | Total complaints / Sworn officers | Track year-over-year |

### Red Flags

- **Sustained rate < 5%** - May indicate inadequate investigation
- **No discipline after sustained findings** - Accountability gap
- **Demographics unavailable** - Cannot assess disparate impact
- **Incomplete years** - Possible data management issues
- **Dramatic year-over-year swings** - Investigate policy or leadership changes

## Escalation Pathways

If IA statistics reveal concerns:

1. **File complaint with Colorado POST** (for decertification)
   - https://post.colorado.gov/public-complaints

2. **Contact Colorado Attorney General** (for Pattern & Practice investigation)
   - Civil Rights Unit: 720-508-6000
   - Required: 5+ complaints or systemic evidence

3. **Present to City Council** during public comment
   - Cite specific statistics and comparisons

4. **Share with media** for investigative coverage
   - Pueblo Chieftain, Colorado Public Radio, Denverite
