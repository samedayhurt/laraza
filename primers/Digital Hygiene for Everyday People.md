# Digital Hygiene for Everyday People

*A simple guide for protecting yourself, your family, and your community online*

*Updated: November 2025*

This guide is for anyone who wants to stay safer online—you don't need to be a tech expert. These are practical steps that take minutes, not hours.

---

## Why This Matters Now

In 2025, your digital footprint can be used against you:
- **ICE monitors social media** 24/7 including Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp
- **License plate readers** track vehicle movements across Colorado
- **Police drones** can respond to incidents in 60-90 seconds
- **Your phone** constantly shares your location

**The good news:** Simple habits can significantly reduce your exposure.

---

## Quick Wins (Do These Today)

### 1. Lock Your Phone Properly
**Time: 2 minutes**

- Use a 6+ digit PIN (not 4 digits, not a pattern)
- Turn OFF fingerprint/face unlock if going to a protest or sensitive area
- Why: Police can force you to use your face/finger, but can't force you to give up a PIN

**Android users:** Enable Advanced Protection
- Settings → Security → Advanced Protection → Enable
- This adds extra security features designed for journalists and activists

### 2. Turn Off Location on Social Media
**Time: 5 minutes**

**Facebook:**
- Settings → Privacy → Location → Turn OFF

**Instagram:**
- Settings → Privacy → Location Services → Never

**TikTok:**
- Settings → Privacy → Location Services → Off

**Why:** Location data in posts tells ICE exactly where you are and when.

### 3. Use Signal Instead of WhatsApp
**Time: 10 minutes to set up**

- Download Signal from your app store
- Signal is encrypted AND doesn't share data with government
- WhatsApp is encrypted BUT owned by Meta and ICE can request metadata

**Signal settings to enable:**
- Disappearing messages: ON (set to 1 week or less)
- Screen security: ON (prevents screenshots)
- Registration lock: ON (prevents account hijacking)

## Protecting Your Family

### Create a Family Emergency Plan
If a family member is detained:
1. **Don't panic.** They have rights.
2. **Call CORRN:** 844-864-8341 (press 1 for live help)
3. **Call RMIAN Detention Hotline:** (303) 866-9308
4. **Have ready:** Full name, date of birth, A-number if known

### Teach Your Kids
- Don't post your address online
- Don't tag your school in posts
- Be careful about what friends tag you in
- If someone asks personal questions online, tell a parent

### Protect Elders
- Help them review privacy settings on Facebook
- Show them how to recognize scam calls/texts
- Set up their phones with longer PINs
- Add CORRN number to their contacts: 844-864-8341

## Social Media Safety

### What NOT to Post
- Your home address or workplace
- Photos of your ID, documents, or mail
- Your daily routine (e.g., "heading to work at 7am every day!")
- Photos that show your street/house number
- Check-ins at sensitive locations (churches, clinics, schools)
- Political organizing details—use Signal instead

### Clean Up Old Posts
- Delete posts with location data
- Remove old photos showing your home
- Untag yourself from photos at sensitive locations
- Review what friends have tagged you in

### Consider a Separate Account
If you're active in organizing:
- Create a separate account with a nickname
- Use a different email (create a new one)
- Don't connect it to your phone number
- Don't follow/friend your real account

## Phone Security Basics

### Before Going Out

**For everyday safety:**
- Make sure your phone is updated (Settings → Software Update)
- Use a strong PIN (6+ digits)
- Know where your phone's "Emergency SOS" feature is

**For protests or sensitive situations:**
- Turn off Face ID / fingerprint unlock
- Enable airplane mode when not needed
- Consider bringing a burner phone instead
- Use a Faraday sleeve/bag to block signals

### If Your Phone is Seized
- Do NOT unlock it for anyone
- Say: "I do not consent to a search"
- Provide your PIN only if ordered by a judge
- Contact a lawyer immediately

## Passwords & Accounts

### Password Rules
- Use different passwords for important accounts (email, banking)
- Make them long (12+ characters) rather than complicated
- A phrase like "MyDogLoves2PlayOutside!" is better than "P@ssw0rd!"
- Write them down on paper and keep it safe—this is better than reusing passwords

### Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)
Turn this on for:
- Your email (most important!)
- Banking
- Social media

**How:** Usually found in Settings → Security → Two-Factor Authentication

### Check for Breaches
- Go to haveibeenpwned.com
- Enter your email address
- If it appears in breaches, change those passwords

## Safe Browsing

### Use a Privacy-Focused Browser
- **Firefox** with privacy settings turned up
- **Brave** blocks ads and trackers automatically
- **Tor Browser** for maximum privacy (slower but very private)

### Browser Settings to Change
- Block third-party cookies
- Turn off "personalized ads"
- Use DuckDuckGo instead of Google for searches

### VPN (Virtual Private Network)
A VPN hides your internet activity from your internet provider.

**Free options:**
- Proton VPN (free tier available, Swiss company)

**Paid options ($3-5/month):**
- Proton VPN (recommended)
- Mullvad (accepts cash, no account needed)

**Avoid:** Free VPNs from unknown companies—they may sell your data.

## Know Your Rights

### If Police Stop You
- You have the right to remain silent
- You have the right to refuse searches
- Say: "I do not consent to a search"
- You can record police in public in Colorado

### If ICE Comes to Your Door
1. **You do NOT have to open the door**
2. Ask through the door: "Do you have a warrant signed by a judge?"
3. If no warrant: "I choose not to open the door"
4. Do not sign anything
5. Call CORRN: **844-864-8341**

### November 2025 Update
A federal judge ruled ICE cannot make warrantless arrests in Colorado based solely on immigration status. Know this. Carry a printed copy.

## For Community Organizers

### Meeting Security
- Use Signal for organizing, not Facebook/WhatsApp
- Enable disappearing messages
- Don't discuss specific plans on social media
- Use in-person meetings for sensitive topics

### Event Planning
- Assume police/ICE monitor public Facebook events
- Use invite-only Signal groups for details
- Designate someone to watch for police/ICE at events
- Have a legal observer with video ready

### If Something Happens
1. **Document:** Video, photos, badge numbers
2. **Hash immediately:** `sha256sum filename` creates proof of original
3. **Report:** File complaint at pueblo.us/449/Internal-Affairs-Section
4. **Escalate:** DA → POST → Attorney General if needed
