# Recording Rights in Colorado

Your legal rights to document police encounters, protests, and public events in Colorado. This guide covers what you can record, how to do it safely, and what to do if police interfere.

---

## The Bottom Line

**Colorado is a one-party consent state.** You can record any conversation you're part of without telling anyone else.

**You have an explicit legal right to record police.** Colorado law specifically protects recording law enforcement officers performing their duties.

---

## Your Legal Rights

### Recording Police Officers

Colorado law (C.R.S. § 24-31-902) explicitly protects your right to record police:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                  YOUR RIGHT TO RECORD POLICE                     │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│                                                                  │
│  YOU MAY:                                                        │
│  ✓ Record any incident involving a police officer                │
│  ✓ Record without asking permission                              │
│  ✓ Record from any public place                                  │
│  ✓ Record openly or discreetly                                   │
│  ✓ Keep your recording device                                    │
│                                                                  │
│  POLICE MAY NOT:                                                 │
│  ✗ Order you to stop recording                                   │
│  ✗ Seize your device without a warrant                          │
│  ✗ Delete your recordings                                        │
│  ✗ Arrest you solely for recording                              │
│  ✗ Retaliate against you for recording                          │
│                                                                  │
│  ONE LIMIT:                                                      │
│  • You cannot physically interfere with officers                 │
│    performing their duties                                       │
│                                                                  │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Remedies If Police Interfere

If an officer violates your recording rights, you can:

1. **File a complaint** with the law enforcement agency
2. **If denied relief**, file a civil lawsuit
3. **Recover damages including:**
   - $500 for each damaged or destroyed recording
   - Replacement value of your device
   - Attorney's fees
   - Punitive damages

### One-Party Consent for Audio

Colorado follows the **one-party consent rule**:

| Scenario | Can You Record? |
|----------|-----------------|
| Conversation you're part of | **Yes** - you're the consenting party |
| Phone call you're on | **Yes** - you're the consenting party |
| Conversation between others in public | **Yes** - no expectation of privacy in public |
| Private conversation between others | **No** - this is eavesdropping |

**Warning**: If you call someone in a **two-party consent state** (California, Florida, etc.), their state's stricter law may apply.

## Recording Techniques

### Best Practices

```
RECORDING CHECKLIST
===================

BEFORE:
□ Fully charge your phone
□ Clear storage space (at least 2GB free)
□ Turn off notifications (prevent interruptions)
□ Enable cloud backup (Google Photos, iCloud)
□ Know your unlock method (use PIN, not face/fingerprint)

DURING:
□ Announce you're recording (optional but can deter misconduct)
□ Keep your hands visible
□ Don't physically interfere
□ Capture badge numbers and faces
□ Record continuously - don't stop/start
□ Capture audio context (what's being said)
□ Note time, location, and officer names

AFTER:
□ Do NOT delete anything
□ Back up immediately to cloud
□ Save original (don't edit)
□ Note witnesses' contact info
□ Write down what happened while fresh
```

### Video-Only Recording (Extra Caution)

If you want to record without capturing your own voice (to avoid any consent issues):

1. **Turn off microphone** in camera settings
2. **Use a dedicated video recorder** app with audio disabled
3. **Keep a second audio recorder** running for your own record

### Positioning for Safety

```
SAFE RECORDING POSITIONS
========================

         ┌─────────────────┐
         │   INCIDENT      │
         │   HAPPENING     │
         └────────┬────────┘
                  │
    ┌─────────────┼─────────────┐
    │             │             │
  GOOD          GOOD          GOOD
Position 1    Position 2    Position 3
(sidewalk)   (across       (elevated
             street)        balcony)

Key principles:
• Stay on public property
• Maintain safe distance (10-15 feet minimum)
• Don't block officers' movement
• Have clear exit route
• Multiple angles are better
```

## If Police Confront You

### What To Say

| Police Action | Your Response |
|---------------|---------------|
| "Stop recording" | "I have a legal right to record under Colorado law." |
| "Give me your phone" | "I do not consent to a search. Do you have a warrant?" |
| "You're interfering" | Step back, but continue recording. "I'm not interfering." |
| "Delete that video" | "I do not consent. I have a right to keep my recording." |
| Grab for your phone | Do not physically resist. Say "I do not consent." |

### What NOT To Do

- **Don't argue or escalate** - record and let lawyers handle it later
- **Don't physically resist** if they take your device
- **Don't unlock your phone** if seized - stay silent
- **Don't delete recordings** - this could be destruction of evidence
- **Don't lie** about whether you're recording

### If Your Device Is Seized

1. **Clearly state**: "I do not consent to this seizure."
2. **Note**: Time, officer name/badge, reason given
3. **Ask**: "Am I being detained or am I free to go?"
4. **Get**: Receipt or property voucher if possible
5. **Immediately**: Contact a lawyer and file a complaint

## Cloud Backup Setup

Set up automatic backup BEFORE you need it:

### iPhone
1. Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud → Photos
2. Enable "iCloud Photos"
3. Also enable "Upload to My Photo Stream"

### Android
1. Open Google Photos app
2. Tap profile picture → Photos settings → Backup
3. Enable "Backup" toggle
4. Set "Backup quality" to "Original"

## Special Situations

### Recording at Protests

```
PROTEST RECORDING OPSEC
=======================

□ Use secondary/burner phone if possible
□ Disable biometric unlock (use PIN only)
□ Turn off location services for camera app
□ Disable lock screen notifications
□ Consider: phone in Faraday bag when not recording
□ Strip metadata before sharing publicly
□ Blur faces of other protesters before posting
□ Don't post real-time (wait until event ends)
```

### Recording Inside Buildings

| Location | Can You Record? |
|----------|-----------------|
| Public sidewalks | Yes - always |
| Public parks | Yes - always |
| Government buildings (lobby) | Usually yes |
| Courtrooms | No - prohibited by most courts |
| Private businesses | Owner can ask you to leave |
| Schools | Varies - check local policy |
| Your own home | Yes - always |

### Recording ICE Encounters

**YES, you can record ICE agents** - they are federal law enforcement, and your 1st Amendment rights apply.

Additional tips for ICE encounters:
- Don't open the door to record through it
- Record through windows if possible
- Capture vehicle plates and agent badges
- Document time agents arrive and leave
- Don't answer questions while recording (5th Amendment)

## After Recording: What To Do

### Preserve Evidence

1. **Save original file** - never edit the source video
2. **Make copies** in multiple locations:
   - Cloud backup
   - External hard drive
   - Encrypted USB drive
3. **Document chain of custody** - note when/where you saved it

### Report Misconduct

**Pueblo Police Department Internal Affairs:**
- File complaint within 60 days
- Request complaint number in writing
- Follow up if no response in 30 days

**Colorado POST (Peace Officer Standards and Training):**
- For serious misconduct
- Can decertify officers
- https://post.colorado.gov

**ACLU of Colorado:**
- Report at aclu-co.org/report
- They track patterns and may take cases
- 303-777-5482

### Share Responsibly

Before posting recordings publicly:

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Blur faces of bystanders/protesters | Post unredacted video identifying protesters |
| Strip metadata (location, time) | Include identifying information |
| Wait until situation resolves | Post real-time during ongoing incident |
| Save original privately | Only keep edited version |
| Consider impact on subjects | Assume everyone wants to be filmed |

### If You're Arrested for Recording

1. **Stay silent** except to ask for a lawyer
2. **Don't unlock your phone** - police cannot compel your PIN (5th Amendment)
3. **Don't consent to searches**
4. **Contact ACLU hotline**: 303-777-5482
5. **Document everything** as soon as released
