# Journalist, Activist, and Protestor Protection Guide

*Updated: November 27, 2025*

This guide distills the latest surveillance, immigration, and resource research into actionable steps for anyone documenting or participating in Pueblo actions.

## Who This Serves
- **Journalists & documentarians** covering city council, protests, or community safety
- **Activists & organizers** coordinating marches, canvasses, or rapid-response calls
- **Legal observers & community medics** documenting abuse and routing survivors to help

## Top Risks Right Now (November 2025)

### 1. Expanded RTCC Surveillance
- **Drones:** 60-90 second response time; assume aerial coverage at any gathering
- **73 mobile ALPRs:** Every Sheriff patrol car scans plates; county-wide movement tracking
- **Park cameras:** 5 major parks now have RTCC-connected surveillance
- **Community Connect Phase 2:** Private cameras now live-streaming to police

### 2. Digital Surveillance Expansion
- **ICE 24/7 social media monitoring:** Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp, YouTube all monitored
- **Flock ALPR protest tracking:** EFF documented nationwide use for surveilling activists
- **Palantir ImmigrationOS:** $30M AI platform integrating multiple data streams for ICE

### 3. Immigration Enforcement Escalation
- ICE arrests quadrupled in Colorado (2025 vs 2024)
- Sensitive areas (schools, churches, hospitals) no longer protected
- Huerfano County detention facility may activate 50 miles south

### 4. Political Landscape
- City Council rejected pro-immigrant resolution 5-2 (Nov 2025)
- Conservative majority continues fast-tracking surveillance budgets
- Council approved 7-0 park surveillance ($1M for cameras, ALPRs at 5 parks)
- Speed camera proposal (15 cameras) pending; monitor upcoming votes

## Critical Updates for Field Safety

### November 2025 Court Victory
Federal judge ordered ICE to stop warrantless arrests in Colorado. **Carry this information:**
> "On November 25, 2025, a federal court ruled ICE cannot make warrantless arrests in Colorado based solely on suspected immigration status."

### Sheriff Lucero's Pledge
PCSO will NOT participate in ICE roundups. Only cooperates on criminal matters. **Carry printed copies** to cite if deputies appear alongside ICE.

## Self-Advocacy & Safety Moves

### Before the Action
- [ ] **Map exposure:** Review `docs/maps/pueblo-surveillance-map.html` for camera clusters, ALPRs, mobile trailers
- [ ] **Check park coverage:** City Park, Mineral Palace, Ray Aguilera, Lake Minnequa, Mitchell Park all have RTCC cameras
- [ ] **Prep case IDs:** Use `YYYYMMDD-Event-Initials` format across all documentation
- [ ] **Social media hygiene:** Remove location data, review privacy settings, use Signal not WhatsApp
- [ ] **Know officials:** Print power map with council member positions on surveillance/immigration
- [ ] **Android users:** Enable Advanced Protection mode (Settings > Security > Advanced Protection)

### During the Action
- [ ] **Document + hash:** Record short clips focused on badges; run `sha256sum` immediately
- [ ] **Text hashes to legal:** Send hash values over Signal so evidence integrity is established
- [ ] **Verify rumors:** Before blasting alerts, call CORRN 844-864-8341 opt.1 with vehicle/address details
- [ ] **Deploy resource cards:** Keep copies of contact table for medics/legal observers
- [ ] **Watch for drones:** RTCC drone program responds in 60-90 seconds; adjust if spotted

### After the Action
- [ ] **File complaints fast:** Submit IA forms same day at pueblo.us/449/Internal-Affairs-Section
- [ ] **Escalate if needed:** DA → POST → AG Pattern & Practice
- [ ] **Track referrals:** Log who contacted which org and outcomes
- [ ] **Update data files:** Append new sightings to `data/*_raw` files for next action
- [ ] **Debrief digital:** Check if any social media posts revealed location; adjust practices

## Digital Hygiene Essentials (2025)

### Phone Security
- **Android:** Enable Advanced Protection (Settings > Security > Advanced Protection)
  - USB protection blocks physical attacks
  - Auto-restart after 72 hours keeps data encrypted
- **All phones:** Disable location services before actions
- **Faraday sleeves:** Use when entering sensitive areas

### Secure Communications
| Use | Avoid |
|-----|-------|
| Signal | WhatsApp (ICE monitors) |
| Element/Matrix | Facebook Messenger |
| Session | Instagram DMs |
| Tor Browser | Regular browsers for research |

### Social Media Discipline
- Remove location data from all posts
- Disable photo location tagging
- Review tagged photos from others
- Use aliases for activist accounts
- Assume ICE monitors: Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Twitter/X, YouTube, Reddit

### VPN Recommendations
- **Proton VPN:** Open source, audited (Sept 2025), Swiss jurisdiction
- **Mullvad:** No account required, accepts cash
- **Avoid:** Free VPNs, VPNs in 14 Eyes countries
