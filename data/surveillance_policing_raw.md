## 2025-11-15 — RTCC sensor stack confirmed via Daktronics release
- **Date / time:** 2025-11-15 desk research
- **Location:** Pueblo PD Real-Time Crime Center
- **Source / doc link:** Daktronics press release, Sept 25 2025 (opsec/Pueblo 81008 Surveillance.md reference)
- **Observed capability:** RTCC integrates ShotSpotter acoustic alerts, body-worn camera feeds, drones, fixed surveillance cameras, and ALPR streams all displayed on a 7’×12’ Daktronics dvLED wall managed by High Point Networks.
- **Immediate risk + mitigation idea:** Centralized telemetry plus third-party sensors increases false positive risk and discovery hurdles. Need CORA requests for vendor SLAs + uptime logs; ensure organizers note RTCC coverage on march routes.

- **Date / time:** 2025-11-15 desk research

## 2025-11-15 — Flock Safety ALPR corridor funded by Downtown Association
- **Date / time:** 2025-11-15 desk research
- **Location:** Union Ave & Riverwalk corridors
- **Source / doc link:** KOAA News5 report, 2024 (docs/pueblo-watchlist.md reference)
- **Observed capability:** Flock cameras capture all plates, run hotlists, store encrypted footage for 30 days, and provide audit logs. Downtown Association paid for first cameras.
- **Immediate risk + mitigation idea:** Potential expansion into residential 81008 without debate. Action: request placement maps from CDOT/city, monitor for agenda items expanding ALPR budgets, coordinate with civil-liberties groups referencing Libertarian dissent.

## 2025-11-15 — Complaint escalation workflow needs documentation packs
- **Date / time:** 2025-11-15 desk research
- **Location:** Pueblo PD Internal Affairs / Colorado POST
- **Source / doc link:** Pueblo PD Internal Affairs page + Colorado POST complaint guidance
- **Observed capability:** IA accepts multi-channel complaints; POST only revokes certs after criminal findings; AG Pattern & Practice form handles systemic issues.
- **Immediate risk + mitigation idea:** Survivors often stop at IA when cases stall. Build template packets (badges, hashes, witness logs) and escalate to DA/AG sooner; track submissions in Obsidian.

## 2025-11-15 — Commercial telemetry + vendor data flows
- **Date / time:** 2025-11-15 desk research
- **Location:** Citywide / vendor platforms
- **Source / doc link:** opsec/Pueblo 81008 Surveillance.md (Commercial Telemetry & Data Broker Touchpoints section).
- **Observed capability:** ShotSpotter alerts processed by private vendor first, Genetec cloud video exports footage outside local control, and Flock Safety stores ALPR hits for 30 days while logging every officer query.
- **Immediate risk + mitigation idea:** Data lives on third-party infrastructure subject to vendor policies/subpoenas. Need public-facing MOUs that define retention, deletion, and audit access; incorporate vendor accountability demands into council testimony.

## 2025-11-15 — Colorado AG Pattern & Practice intake
- **Date / time:** 2025-11-15 desk research
- **Location:** Statewide (Colorado Attorney General)
- **Source / doc link:** Colorado Attorney General Pattern & Practice Program (https://coag.gov/office-sections/civil-rights/pattern-and-practice/).
- **Observed capability:** AG’s Civil Rights Division accepts online complaints against governmental authorities (including police departments) alleging systemic civil-rights violations; submissions can include attachments, witness info, and agency details.
- **Immediate risk + mitigation idea:** Few Pueblo residents escalate beyond IA/POST; we need to package evidence (videos, hashes, statements) to send through this form whenever multiple incidents occur so the AG can investigate patterns.
