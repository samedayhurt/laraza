## 2025-08 — Flock-ICE Scandal: 1,400+ Immigration Searches via Colorado ALPR Network
- **Date / time:** June 2024 - April 2025 (Denver audit logs), August 2025 CORA release
- **Location:** Statewide Colorado Flock network (Denver 111 cameras, potential Pueblo access)
- **Source / doc link:** Colorado Newsline (Aug 6, 2025); ACLU Colorado coalition statement (Aug 2025); Sentinel Colorado (2025).
- **Observed capability:** Out-of-state agencies + Border Patrol ran 1,400+ searches with "ICE"/"immigration" justifications via Denver's national lookup feature. 690 searches post-Trump inauguration. Loveland PD admitted sharing portal with ICE federal agent. Pueblo's Downtown Association Flock cameras (2022 deployment) potentially accessible via national network unless disabled.
- **Immediate risk + mitigation idea:** Immigrant community members' movements tracked via ALPR network accessible to ICE. Action: (1) CORA Pueblo PD to confirm if national lookup enabled on local Flock cameras; (2) demand City Council resolution prohibiting ICE data sharing (precedent: Denver disabled April 2025, Boulder June 2025); (3) organize with ACLU-CO and immigrant rights groups.

## 2025 — Colorado ALPR Legislation Pending: Sen. Amabile Warrant Requirement
- **Date / time:** 2025 legislative session (under consideration)
- **Location:** Statewide Colorado
- **Source / doc link:** CBS Colorado (2025).
- **Observed capability:** Proposed law would require warrant based on probable cause for police to share ALPR data with other agencies (parallel to GPS/cellphone tracking requirements). Limits out-of-state and internal access.
- **Immediate risk + mitigation idea:** Current lack of regulation allows warrantless ALPR searches and ICE data sharing. Action: (1) Organize community testimony supporting Sen. Amabile's bill; (2) coordinate with ACLU-CO and EFF on legislative advocacy; (3) push Pueblo City Council to adopt Boulder PD model (30-day retention, no national lookup, supervisory approval for external sharing).

## 2025 — Pueblo Police Racial Disparities: 2.8x Black, 2x Latinx Killing Rate (2013-23)
- **Date / time:** 2013-2023 data, published 2025
- **Location:** Pueblo Police Department jurisdiction
- **Source / doc link:** Police Scorecard: Pueblo, CO (policescorecard.org/co/police-department/pueblo).
- **Observed capability:** Based on population, Black persons 2.8x more likely and Latinx persons 2x more likely to be killed by police than white persons. 57% of arrests for low-level, non-violent offenses (potential over-policing). Pueblo shows more racial disparities in deadly force than 54% of departments evaluated.
- **Immediate risk + mitigation idea:** Surveillance tech (ShotSpotter, ALPR, RTCC) disproportionately impacts communities of color. Action: (1) Demand demographic impact analysis before surveillance expansions; (2) push for independent civilian oversight commission (precedent: Colorado Springs 2025); (3) CORA complaint data + discipline records; (4) package patterns for CO AG Pattern & Practice complaint.
