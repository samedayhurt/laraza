## 2025-11-15 — Colorado Legal Services (Southeastern Colorado / Pueblo office)
- **Organization:** Colorado Legal Services — Southeastern Colorado Office (Pueblo)
- **Service focus:** Free civil legal aid for low-income residents (eviction defense, domestic violence protection orders, consumer issues, immigration screenings).
- **Contact / intake:** 1000 W. 6th St., Suite I, Pueblo, CO 81003; phone 719-545-6708; online intake via https://www.coloradolegalservices.org/node/10/pueblo-office.
- **Eligibility / notes:** Income + asset screening; bilingual staff available; priority for survivors of violence, seniors, and people facing housing loss.
- **Source / verification date:** Colorado Legal Services website, accessed 2025-11-15.

## 2025-11-15 — Posada Pueblo
- **Organization:** Posada
- **Service focus:** Housing navigation, emergency motel vouchers, permanent supportive housing, mail services, and case management for families, veterans, and youth experiencing homelessness in Pueblo County.
- **Contact / intake:** 1008 N. Grand Ave., Pueblo, CO 81003; phone 719-545-8776; intake form at https://posadapueblo.org/services/.
- **Eligibility / notes:** Serves households experiencing or at risk of homelessness; offers bilingual services; coordinates with Pueblo County’s Continuum of Care.
- **Source / verification date:** Posada Pueblo website, accessed 2025-11-15.

## 2025-11-27 — Mariposa Center for Safety (formerly YWCA Pueblo) — UPDATED
- **Organization:** Mariposa Center for Safety (rebranded from YWCA Pueblo in 2023)
- **Service focus:** 24/7 domestic violence crisis line, emergency shelter beds, legal advocacy, court accompaniment, economic empowerment classes.
- **Contact / intake:** **24/7 Crisis Line: 719-545-8195** (CORRECTED); Office: 719-542-6904; Address: 801 N. Santa Fe Avenue, Pueblo, CO 81003; Email: shelterywca@ywcapueblo.com.
- **Eligibility / notes:** Serves all survivors regardless of gender; capacity-based shelter placement; can coordinate safe transportation within Pueblo County. Languages: English, Spanish. Received $80,000 Colorado Homeland Security grant (2025) to strengthen 24/7 crisis hotline.
- **Source / verification date:** Mariposa Center for Safety / YWCA Pueblo website; KOAA (2025); verified 2025-11-27.

## 2025-11-27 — Juniper Southern Colorado / Pueblo Rape Crisis Services — UPDATED
- **Organization:** Juniper Southern Colorado (formerly Pueblo Rape Crisis Services)
- **Service focus:** 24/7 sexual assault crisis hotline (CALL OR TEXT), hospital and court accompaniment, long-term case management, counseling referrals, prevention education.
- **Contact / intake:** **24/7 Crisis Hotline: 719-549-0549 (call OR text)** — NEW; Office: 719-544-1191; Address: 503 N Main St, Suite 526, Pueblo, CO 81003; website https://rapecrisisservices.org/.
- **Eligibility / notes:** ONLY sexual violence resource center serving Pueblo and surrounding counties (since mid-1970s). Serves 450+ individuals annually. Spanish-speaking advocates available on request.
- **Source / verification date:** Juniper Southern Colorado / Rape Crisis Services website; ColoradoGives; verified 2025-11-27.

## 2025-11-15 — Health Solutions Crisis Walk-In Center
- **Organization:** Health Solutions
- **Service focus:** Behavioral health crisis stabilization (24/7 walk-in), peer support, detox linkage, and mobile crisis response for Pueblo County.
- **Contact / intake:** 711 W. 13th St., Pueblo, CO 81003; crisis line 800-493-8255 (Colorado Crisis Services); https://www.health.solutions/services/crisis-care/.
- **Eligibility / notes:** No-cost crisis stabilization regardless of insurance; coordinates transport for youth and adults; integrates with Pueblo Police co-responder teams.
- **Source / verification date:** Health Solutions website, accessed 2025-11-15.

## 2025-11-15 — Colorado Rapid Response Network (CORRN)
- **Organization:** Colorado Rapid Response Network
- **Service focus:** Statewide hotline that dispatches trained legal observers and documentarians to immigration enforcement activity; logs past incidents for follow-up.
- **Contact / intake:** Hotline 844-864-8341 (option 1 live, option 2 past incidents); https://www.coloradorapidresponsenetwork.com/
- **Eligibility / notes:** Anyone witnessing ICE/police collaboration or immigration enforcement in Colorado; bilingual dispatchers coordinate with Colorado Immigrant Rights Coalition chapters.
- **Source / verification date:** CORRN website, accessed 2025-11-15.

## 2025-11-15 — Rocky Mountain Immigrant Advocacy Network (RMIAN)
- **Organization:** Rocky Mountain Immigrant Advocacy Network
- **Service focus:** Free legal orientation, direct representation, and social service navigation for detained adults (Aurora GEO) and unaccompanied children statewide.
- **Contact / intake:** https://rmian.org/get-help/; adult detention hotline 303-433-2812; child program 303-222-0003.
- **Eligibility / notes:** Colorado residents with family members in immigration detention or unaccompanied minors; requires A-number and detention location for intake.
- **Source / verification date:** RMIAN website, accessed 2025-11-15.

## 2025-11-15 — Colorado Crisis Services (statewide hotline)
- **Organization:** Colorado Crisis Services
- **Service focus:** 24/7 statewide mental health, substance-use, and emotional support hotline/text line with mobile response coordination.
- **Contact / intake:** Hotline 844-493-8255; text “TALK” to 38255; walk-in centers listed at https://coloradocrisisservices.org/.
- **Eligibility / notes:** Available to anyone in Colorado regardless of insurance or immigration status; can conference in translators and connect callers to local crisis centers like Health Solutions.
- **Source / verification date:** Colorado Crisis Services website, accessed 2025-11-15.
