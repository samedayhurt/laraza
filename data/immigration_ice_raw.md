## 2025-01-24 — ICE “roundup” rumors traced to targeted arrest
- **Date / trigger:** 2025-01-24 social media rumors
- **Agency or facility:** ICE & local police collaboration
- **Summary:** Colorado Public Radio reported statewide panic over alleged ICE “roundups,” including Pueblo sightings that turned out to be ICE assisting a narcotics arrest. Advocates still logged door-knock claims and stressed rapid verification before amplifying alerts.
- **Sources:** Caitlyn Kim, “Rumors of immigration arrests spread across Colorado, but details are unclear,” CPR News, Jan 24 2025.
- **Suggested response / referral:** Encourage community members to verify sightings via CORRN hotline before sharing; prep template messages clarifying what was confirmed in Pueblo.

## 2025-01-23 — Sheriff Lucero limits cooperation with ICE
- **Date / trigger:** 2025-01-23 statement release
- **Agency or facility:** Pueblo County Sheriff’s Office
- **Summary:** Sheriff David Lucero issued a public letter stating PCSO “will not support or participate in any round-up operations” and only cooperates with ICE when there are criminal charges or officer-safety justifications.
- **Sources:** Pueblo County Sheriff’s Office news release, “Sheriff Lucero Issues Statement on Agency’s Cooperation with ICE,” Jan 23 2025.
- **Suggested response / referral:** Carry printouts of the statement to cite when deputies attempt to assist ICE at schools/churches; log any deviations for Pattern & Practice complaints.

## 2025-11-15 — Colorado Rapid Response Network hotline + documentation workflow
- **Date / trigger:** Standing resource check
- **Agency or facility:** Colorado Rapid Response Network (CORRN)
- **Summary:** CORRN runs a statewide hotline (844-864-8341) dispatching trained legal observers for active ICE activity (option 1) and recording past incidents (option 2). They coordinate with Colorado Immigrant Rights Coalition chapters to map raids and mobilize legal support.
- **Sources:** CORRN website (accessed 2025-11-15); Colorado Immigrant Rights Coalition resources page.
- **Suggested response / referral:** Program hotline into protest/legal observer phones; pair calls with rapid documentation (badge numbers, photos) and share with CIRC for follow-up clinics.
